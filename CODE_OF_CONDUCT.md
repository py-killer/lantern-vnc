# Community code of conduct

Lantern's community should be useful and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of technical experience.

Treat others with respect. Discuss the behavior of the software and the substance of an idea. Give reproducible examples, accept corrections, and assume that a short message may be missing context.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized behavior, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not post passwords, private connection details, or personal information in issues, pull requests, or screenshots.

This conduct applies in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions, wiki, and other project-managed spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close a conversation, or restrict participation when needed. Responses should reflect the behavior and its impact rather than a person's identity or viewpoint.

For a conduct concern, ask a maintainer for a private contact route using a brief, nonsensitive message. A dedicated confidential reporting address is not currently published. Avoid naming affected people or sharing evidence publicly while requesting that route. For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
